USSSA Account Deletion

```

USSSA users can request deletion of their USSSA account and associated personal data.

How to delete your account

Users can delete their account directly from the USSSA mobile application:

  1. Open the USSSA application.
  2. Sign in to your USSSA account.
  3. Open your account or profile settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the information displayed on the confirmation screen.
  6. Confirm the account deletion.

Request account deletion

If you cannot access the USSSA application, you can request account deletion by contacting USSSA support.

Please provide the email address associated with your USSSA account so that your account can be identified.

Data deletion

When an account deletion request is processed, USSSA will delete the user data associated with the account that is eligible for deletion.

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.
```
