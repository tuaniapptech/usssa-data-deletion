USSSA Account Deletion

This page provides information about how to delete your USSSA account and associated data.

Delete Your Account in the USSSA App

USSSA users can delete their account directly from the USSSA mobile application.

  1. Open the USSSA application.
  2. Sign in to your USSSA account.
  3. Open your account or profile settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the information displayed on the confirmation screen.
  6. Confirm the account deletion.

Request Account Deletion

If you cannot delete your account directly from the USSSA application, you can request deletion of your USSSA account and associated data by sending an email to:

helpdesk@ussssa.com

Please use the email address associated with your USSSA account when submitting your request.

Subject: USSSA Account Deletion Request

Data Deletion

When an account deletion request is processed, USSSA will delete the user data associated with the account that is eligible for deletion.

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.

USSSA

This account deletion process is provided for the USSSA mobile application.
