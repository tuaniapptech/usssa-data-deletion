USSSA Photo and Data Deletion

This page provides information about how to delete photos and other data associated with your USSSA account.

Photo Deletion in the USSSA App

USSSA users can delete photos directly from the USSSA mobile application.

  1. Open the USSSA application.
  2. Sign in to your USSSA account.
  3. Open the section containing the photo you want to delete.
  4. Select the photo you want to delete.
  5. Select Delete.
  6. Confirm the photo deletion.

Request Photo or Data Deletion

If you cannot delete a photo directly from the USSSA application, you can request deletion of the photo or associated data by sending an email to:

helpdesk@ussssa.com

Please use the email address associated with your USSSA account and provide enough information for us to identify the photo or data you would like to have deleted.

Subject: USSSA Photo/Data Deletion Request

Data Deletion

USSSA will review valid deletion requests and delete the requested data where applicable.

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.
