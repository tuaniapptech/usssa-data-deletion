USSSA Photo and Data Deletion

```

USSSA users can request deletion of photos and other data associated with their USSSA account.

Photo deletion

If a photo can be deleted directly within the USSSA application, users should use the available deletion option provided by the application.

Request photo or data deletion

If you cannot delete a photo or other user-associated data through the USSSA application, you can contact USSSA support and request deletion.

Please provide enough information to identify the photo or data you would like to have deleted, such as the account email, event, team, or other relevant information.

Data deletion

USSSA will review valid deletion requests and delete the requested data where applicable.

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.

USSSA

This page provides information about photo and data deletion for the USSSA mobile application.
```
