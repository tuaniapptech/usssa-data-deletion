USSSA Photo and Data Deletion

```

This page provides information about how to request deletion of photos and other data associated with your USSSA account.

Request Photo or Data Deletion

To request deletion of a photo or other data associated with your USSSA account, please send an email to:

helpdesk@ussssa.com

Please use the email address associated with your USSSA account and provide enough information for us to identify the photo or data you would like to have deleted.

Subject: USSSA Photo/Data Deletion Request

Photo Deletion in the USSSA App

If a photo deletion option is available in the USSSA application, users can use that option to delete the photo directly.

If you cannot delete the photo through the application, you can submit a deletion request using the email address above.

Data Deletion

USSSA will review valid deletion requests and delete the requested data where applicable.

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.

USSSA

This data deletion process is provided for the USSSA mobile application.
```
